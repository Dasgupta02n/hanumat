# Hanumat against Hindu devotional sites worldwide

**Date:** 6 September 2026  
**Subject:** [hanumat.life](https://hanumat.life/)  
**Question:** Where does Hanumat rank — on **feature, function, aesthetics, content, and experience** — against Hindu scripture, stotra, mandir, calendar, and puja sites globally?

This is a **product ranking**, not a traffic ranking. Reach is reported separately so the two are not mixed. Traffic numbers below are third-party estimates or first-party claims; they are not audited.

**Cross-check (6 Sep 2026):** festival windows in Path Studio’s household panchang were verified against [Drik Panchang Indian Calendar 2026](https://www.drikpanchang.com/calendars/indian/indiancalendar.html) plus a second public calendar (India TV / Hindu Blog / Times of India / Hindustan Times). Reach figures: T-Series 5B ([Livemint](https://www.livemint.com/entertainment/14-years-ago-iconic-gulshan-kumar-video-only-one-in-india-with-over-5-billion-views-shree-hanuman-chalisa-11764216897582.html), [India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/entertainment/music/story/shree-hanuman-chalisa-becomes-first-indian-video-to-cross-5-billion-views-on-youtube-2825956-2025-11-25)); Sri Mandir 3.5M MAU / ~40M downloads as company claim ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/30/sri-mandir-keeps-investors-hooked-as-digital-devotion-grows/), [CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/business/startup/appsforbharat-series-c-funding-sri-mandir-faith-tech-footprint-across-temple-towns-ws-l-19629842.htm)). Gita verse count 701 is this recension (adhyaya 13 = 35); Wikipedia and many printed Gitas count 700.

Hanumat is a **quiet three-dham digital mandir** (Hanuman, Shiva, Kali) with Path Studio: mula, IAST, Hindi/English meaning, japa, parayan, PWA. No ads, no accounts, no paid puja, no astrology.

## 2. Method

### What was scored (equal weight, 1–10)

| Axis | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Features** | Path tools, japa, parayan, PWA, print, IAST, galleries, calendar, audio, scripts |
| **Function** | Does daily sadhana actually work? Login? Paywall? Resume? Mobile? |
| **Aesthetics** | Does it feel like a mandir, a shop, a 1998 archive, or an ad farm? |
| **Content** | Breadth **and** honesty (edition, recension, no fake license, Kali care) |
| **Experience** | Friction + mood: ads, trackers, astrology upsell, paid puja, clutter |

### What was *not* mixed in

- App-store download counts  
- Venture funding  
- Wikipedia notability  
- SEO pageview farms  

Those matter for **reach**. They do not decide whether the mandir is well made.

### Peer set (not “every Hindu URL”)

Hindu web is four jobs. Ranking one list without splitting jobs is how people fool themselves.

| **Library** — thousands of stotras / shastras | Vignanam, Sanskrit Documents, Green Message, StotraNidhi |
| **Calendar / muhurat** | Drik Panchang |
| **Paid puja / temple video** | Sri Mandir |

Inspected live or via primary pages: Hanumat, [Vignanam](https://vignanam.org/), [Green Message](https://greenmesg.org/), [Sanskrit Documents](https://sanskritdocuments.org/), [ramayana.info](https://ramayana.info/), [Drik Panchang](https://www.drikpanchang.com/), [Gita Press](https://www.gitapress.org/), [Sri Mandir](https://www.srimandir.com/) (via TechCrunch + Play listing), holy-bhagavad-gita.org, Vedabase, StotraNidhi (via traffic competitor lists), temples.bio / chalisa SEO sites.

## 5. Axis notes

### Features — Hanumat 9.0

**Has:** Path Studio (mula, IAST, meaning, section/full, focus, print, share, `?verse=`), **Bhagavad Gita 18 adhyaya**, household scripture shelf, japa mala, 7/9/21/40 parayan, courtyard gate, three dhams, 108-image galleries, PWA, local resume/bookmarks, kids / katha / glossary, temple lists, trust / FAQ / for-temples, **household panchang** (IST tithi / paksha / nakshatra / named festivals / month grid / dham path of the day). No kundali, no muhurat consultancy, no ads.

**Does not have (and peers do):**

| Gap | Who has it |
|-----|------------|
| 15–18 Indic scripts on mula | Vignanam (Chalisa in 18 scripts + IAST + bhāvārtha; 32 Hanuman stotras), ramayana.info |
| City-level tithi / muhurat | Drik Panchang |
| Live temple puja + chadhava | Sri Mandir |
| Verse audio + acharya ṭīkā | holy-bhagavad-gita.org |
| Thousands of stotras | Vignanam, Sanskrit Documents |

Audio was removed from Hanumat by product choice (September 2026). That is an honest loss for families who recited by ear. It is a gain for honesty (no TTS passing as pāṭh).

**Path-mandir feature rank: 1st. All-Hindu feature rank: 1st** if “feature” means *sit and recite plus know the day without an ad wall*. Drik still has more *panchang fields* (sunrise, city, muhurat).

### Function — Hanumat 9.0

Daily sadhana works without an account: English default, Hindi one tap, verse deep-links, resume, print, WhatsApp, Add to Home Screen. Courtyard and calendar now answer “what do I recite *today*” (vara + tithi + festival → a dham path).

Weaker than Drik for city sunrise / muhurat, weaker than Sri Mandir for “do a ritual in my name,” weaker than Mukundananda’s Gita for “one verse with a teacher.”

Stronger than archive sites (Sanskrit Documents, Vignanam) whose function is *find a file*, not *sit and recite*.

### Aesthetics — Hanumat 9.0

Clearest win. Cream–gold–vermillion tokens, courtyard as a gate, folk leela stills, no “Remove Ads” bar, no kundali widgets.

Green Message is the only peer that also feels like an ashram. Vignanam and Sanskrit Documents are document dumps (intentional, corpus-first). Drik is a dense almanac with ads. Stotra SEO sites are cluttered.

Caveat: 108 galleries are generated folk paintings, not classical pata or temple sculpture. Consistent beauty; not a museum.

### Content — Hanumat 8.5

**Strengths**

- Chalisa, Sundar Kand (Manas), Valmiki Sundarakanda, **Bhagavad Gita (18 adhyaya)**, Shiva core, Kali core, household canon (Gayatri, Lakshmi, Saraswati, Ganesha, Guru, Krishna, Achyutashtakam) — in one Path Studio  
- Vishnu Sahasranama **doorway** (dhyana + 30 names; not a dump of 1,000)  
- Durga Saptashloki (7 verses from Devi Mahatmya; not the full Saptashati)  
- Edition pins; two public recensions named; no fake Gita Press digital license ([Trust](https://hanumat.life/en/trust/), [llms.txt](https://hanumat.life/llms.txt))  
- Kali: public stotra only; no Karpuradi paddhati; no unverified Kali Chalisa  
- IAST on every verse  

**Weaknesses**

- Full Vishnu Sahasranama (1,000 names) and full Durga Saptashati are still not first-class paths  
- Meanings are plain language / provisional — not scholarly ṭīkā (the site says this)  
- Tens of texts (~46 paths), not thousands  
- UI locales are English and Hindi only (Vignanam ~15 scripts)  

Gita Press remains the **authority** on printed Hindu text. Sanskrit Documents and Vignanam remain the **archives**. Hanumat is a **curated recitation mandir** that now includes the Gita.

### Experience — Hanumat 9.0

Experience = friction + mood.

Hanumat: no account, no ads, no tracker pitch, no “book puja,” no sade-sati pack. Courtyard → dham → path.

Sri Mandir is an excellent app and a **commerce surface** (20–25% take rate on temple services; merchandise expanding — TechCrunch). Drik is the Hindu web’s daily utility with a persistent “Remove Ads” control. Those are different ethics.

The no-commerce mandir experience is Hanumat’s actual moat. Do not trade it for reach.

## 6. Peer dossiers (short)

### Hanumat — hanumat.life

Three-dham PWA. Path Studio is the signature. Trust copy is unusually strict for this category. English-default helps diaspora. Corpus is curated, not encyclopedic. Audio removed. Traffic invisible in public rankers.

### holy-bhagavad-gita.org

Best **one-book** Hindu web product: Devanagari, Roman, English, commentary (Swami Mukundananda), audio. ~0.5–0.7M visits/month (Semrush, late 2025). Beats Hanumat on Gita; loses on mandir, multi-deity, japa, parayan, aesthetics.

### ramayana.info

Full Valmiki, 7 kandas, 23,402 shlokas, 7 scripts, daily shloka, pradakshina of 108 names, no ads / no accounts / donations. Closest **ethical** peer. Beats Hanumat on Ramayana completeness and scripts. Loses on Path Studio craft, three-dham architecture, Shiva/Kali, japa mala.

### Green Message — greenmesg.org

Stotras + Bharatavarsha pilgrimage geography + Ramakrishna/Vivekananda/Ramana + nature. Feels like an ashram. Weaker Path Studio; stronger pilgrimage encyclopedia.

### Vaidika Vignanam — vignanam.org

The stotra supermarket: Shiva 100+, Vishnu 200+, Veda, Upanishad, puja vidhanam, 15+ scripts, Android/iOS. UI is a 1990s catalogue. High bounce in some snapshots. Corpus champion; mandir loser.

### Sanskrit Documents — sanskritdocuments.org

Volunteer Devanagari + IAST archive, tools, scanned books. Personal study, not a temple. ~0.9M visits/month; ~90% India. Global rank ~45k (Semrush, Feb 2026). Feature-poor, content-rich.

### Drik Panchang — drikpanchang.com

Hindu calendar OS of the internet. Tithi, nakshatra, muhurat, festivals, kundali, city-level. Ads + optional account. 58M visitors in 2024 (first-party). Different job than Hanumat. If “Hindu websites” means “what Hindus open every morning,” Drik wins.

### Sri Mandir — srimandir.com / com.mandir

India’s leading **Hindu app**. 40M+ downloads, 3.5M MAU, diaspora ARPU ~10× India, $12M run-rate, Series C $20M (Jun 2025). Online puja, chadhava, bhajan/chalisa audio, 70–100+ temples. Functionally excellent. Experientially a marketplace. Hanumat should not copy this model.

### Gita Press — gitapress.org

Not a competitor website so much as the **print ground truth**. 417M+ books; 141M Gita / 108M Manas / 5M Chalisa copies in a 2019 estimate cited by Wikipedia. No ads in publications; subsidised print. Website is commerce + PDFs. Hanumat correctly refuses to claim a Gita Press digital license.

### StotraNidhi and chalisa SEO sites

Highest stotra *traffic*. Lowest mandir *craft*. Ads, thin verification claims, dump layouts. Hanumat should not chase this rank.

## 7. What Hanumat uniquely holds

1. **Courtyard as a daily gate** — three dhams, one URL, last-mandir memory. Almost nobody else is a *place*.  
2. **Path Studio as a product** — verse cards, IAST, meaning locale, focus, print, share, bookmarks, section map. Archives give files; Hanumat gives a recitation room.  
3. **Trust as a feature** — edition pin, dual recension, no GP license lie, no Karpuradi, no unverified Kali Chalisa, no paid puja.  
4. **Aesthetics as seva** — cream-gold-vermillion, folk leela, no scream-Hanuman stock, no “Remove Ads.”  
5. **English-default diaspora UX** without abandoning Hindi mula.

## 8. What would move the rank

To stay #1 in the mandir class **and** climb the all-Hindu list:

1. **Gita + one Vishnu path + one Devi mahatmya** as first-class Path Studio texts. Biggest content gap vs Mukundananda / Vignanam.  
2. **More Indic scripts on mula** (Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Bengali…) without 15 locales of machine meaning. Vignanam’s real advantage.  
3. **Discoverability** — Search Console, temple QR, Tuesday WhatsApp — so product rank can become reach rank. Hanumat already has the on-site kits; the remaining work is operator-side.  
4. **Do not add ads, accounts, or paid puja.** That is the experience lead. Sri Mandir already owns the other model.  
5. Optional later: **commissioned human pāṭh**, labelled as such — not TTS.

## 9. How to read this without self-deception

- **Product #1 in a small class** is true.  
- **Most important Hindu website in the world** is false. That is still Gita Press (print), Drik (daily utility), or Sri Mandir (phone).  
- **Best quiet digital mandir a diaspora family can install** is the claim Hanumat can defend.

## 11. Caveats

- Ranker traffic (Semrush / Similarweb) is modelled, not server logs. Treat as order-of-magnitude.  
- Sites change. Drik and Sri Mandir ship continuously; archives less so.  
- “All Hindu sites in the world” is not enumerable. This briefing covers the **jobs** a devotee actually uses: path, library, calendar, puja.  
- Hanumat content quality varies by text (Chalisa vs wave-1 stotras vs Valmiki sample vs dual-review flags). The 7.0 content score is an average, not a claim that every path is GP-grade.  
- The author of this note also works on Hanumat. Scores on aesthetics and experience are the most at risk of home-team bias; corpus scores were deliberately kept conservative.
- A parallel deep-research pass (6 Sep 2026, status **partial**) independently confirmed: no public visit count for hanumat.life; no store listing; no Wikipedia article; Sri Mandir / Drik / YouTube numbers above; Gita Press is a shop not a recitation studio; priest-path and dosha-puja apps sit at the opposite ethics pole. It did **not** produce a third-party quality league table — the 1–10 scores in §3 are editorial.
- That pass still described Hanumat audio as TTS path-assist, citing a July 2023 vetting file. **Current product (Sep 2026) has no recitation audio.** Treat the older audit as historical.
- IIT Kanpur Valmiki / Gita supersite and Divine Life Society Hanuman URLs did not load cleanly in that pass; they are omitted rather than scored.
- Play Store **10M+** is a bucket; TechCrunch **40M** is a company cumulative claim. Both are cited; neither was re-audited.
